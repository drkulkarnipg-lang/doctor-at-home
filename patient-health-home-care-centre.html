Patient Health & Home Care Centre

Ten connected services to help patients and caregivers understand health information, monitor changes, prepare for care and maintain continuity between hospital, home and doctor.

Clinical safety: This centre is an educational and documentation aid, not a diagnostic service. Scores, alerts and home measurements do not independently determine treatment. Individual targets and care plans must be confirmed by the treating clinician. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, seek emergency medical care immediately.
01

Personal Health Passport

One organised view of the patient’s health history.

  • Diagnoses, operations, admissions and treating teams
  • Current medicines, allergies and reaction details
  • Key reports, devices, baseline function and emergency contacts
  • Patient-approved summary for appointments and referrals

Design requirement: consent-based access, secure storage, export and correction history.

02

Disease-wise Monitoring Centre

Structured follow-up around the condition being monitored.

  • Diabetes: glucose, HbA1c, hypoglycaemia and complication screening
  • Hypertension/CVD: home BP technique, pulse and trend
  • Heart failure: weight, oedema, breathlessness and clinician-set zones
  • COPD/asthma: symptoms, inhaler technique and individual oxygen targets
  • CKD/cirrhosis: relevant laboratory trends, fluid status and medicine review

Monitoring frequency and action thresholds must be individually prescribed.

03

Clinical Warning Signs Encyclopedia

Recognise when a symptom needs urgent assessment.

  • Emergency symptoms: severe breathlessness, chest pain, stroke signs, collapse, major bleeding or anaphylaxis
  • Condition-specific warning signs and deterioration patterns
  • What to observe, what information to share and where to seek help
  • Clear emergency-first guidance; no paywall or AI gate

Each entry requires evidence sources, review date and clinician approval.

04

Medication Safety & Reconciliation Centre

A current, verified medicine list and safer transitions.

  • Medicine name, strength, dose, route, frequency and indication
  • Prescriber, start/stop dates and reason for each change
  • Allergy, reaction, severity and verification status
  • Duplicate therapy, interaction and adherence review prompts
  • Discrepancy resolution documented by the clinician

Never stop, start or change a prescribed medicine solely on an automated prompt.

05

Home Monitoring Calculators

Understand measurements with units, formulas and limitations.

  • BMI, BSA, ideal/adjusted body weight
  • MAP, pulse pressure and orthostatic BP recording
  • eGFR/CrCl and urine albumin ratio interpretation
  • Glucose/eAG and selected electrolyte calculations
  • Selected validated clinical scores with context and caveats

Show input units, formula, reference, limitations and clinician-review warning.

06

Discharge-to-Home Care Toolkit

A safer bridge from hospital discharge to home care.

  • Discharge summary and pending-result checklist
  • Medication reconciliation and follow-up appointments
  • Feeding, catheter, tracheostomy and oxygen safety education
  • Pressure injury prevention, positioning and mobility guidance
  • Wound care, aspiration precautions and escalation contacts

Device-specific instructions must match the patient’s discharge orders and caregiver training.

07

Elderly Care & Frailty Centre

Preserve safety, function, dignity and independence.

  • Falls, gait, balance, frailty and mobility
  • Nutrition, hydration, cognition, mood and sleep
  • ADL/IADL, vision, hearing and social support
  • Polypharmacy, pressure injury and caregiver burden
  • Home hazard and assistive-device review

Assess goals and baseline function; avoid age-only assumptions.

08

Palliative & Symptom Relief Centre

Support comfort, communication and family caregivers.

  • Pain, breathlessness, nausea, constipation and sleep
  • Nutrition, function, emotional and spiritual concerns
  • Patient goals, advance-care discussions and care preferences
  • Caregiver support, respite and planned escalation

Symptom medicines and anticipatory prescriptions require an authorised clinician.

09

Patient & Caregiver Encyclopedia

Understand conditions and care using clear, reviewed resources.

  • Plain-language disease and investigation explainers
  • Preparation for appointments and questions to ask
  • Home-care, diet and medicine-safety education
  • English, Marathi and Hindi initially; reviewed translations
  • References, author/reviewer and revision date on each article

Original educational writing; do not reproduce third-party copyrighted articles.

10

Doctor-reviewed Care Plan Generator

Convert verified clinical information into an individual care plan.

  • Patient goals, diagnoses and relevant history
  • Monitoring tasks, frequency and clinician-set thresholds
  • Medicine list reconciled against the current prescription
  • Caregiver responsibilities, escalation and follow-up
  • Doctor review, approval, date and version before release

AI may organise information; it must not independently diagnose, prescribe or approve the plan.
